KYC's Core Project

Mission
Clean City

Not a campaign. A structured civic operation run by youth to fix a broken sanitation system through direct action, data-backed pressure, and community involvement.

Report an Issue Track Your Complaint
The Six Pillars

What Mission Clean City Actually Does

Each pillar is a concrete, execution-based action — not a slogan.

01

Door-to-Door Waste Awareness

Volunteers directly engage with households across all wards, educating families on wet vs dry waste segregation with hands-on demonstrations. Goal: behavioral change, not just awareness.

02

On-Ground Cleanliness Drives

Regular, scheduled cleaning campaigns in neglected areas — streets, drains, markets, and empty plots. These are continuous operations, not one-time events for social media.

03

Ward-Level Problem Identification

We systematically map every area with zero or poor sanitation service. Every issue is documented with photos, location data, and a written report before any escalation.

04

System Activation (Pressure + Follow-Up)

Using public grievance portals and direct authority channels, we ensure the Nagar Parishad actually responds to every filed complaint — and we track resolution, not just submission.

05

Community Mobilization

Residents, shopkeepers, and students are actively involved — converting passive citizens into responsible participants who take ownership of their local environment.

06

Volunteer Task System

Each volunteer is assigned specific areas and clearly defined tasks. Accountability is tracked — this is how we ensure real ground execution, not just enthusiasm.

Core Strategy

Awareness + Action + Accountability

Not dependent on government

We don't wait for authorities — we force system efficiency through organized pressure.

Data-backed complaints

Every issue filed with photographic evidence, location details, and documented follow-ups.

Visible pressure = faster results

Our organized approach makes ignoring complaints much harder for local authorities.

Continuous, not episodic

Operations run weekly, not once a quarter. Momentum and consistency drive change.

Impact Vision

What Success Looks Like

Every ward gets equal attention

No zone is left behind — all wards receive dedicated volunteer coverage.

Daily waste collection becomes standard

No more multi-day gaps in collection — we push until it's normalized everywhere.

Open dumping eliminated

Unauthorized dump sites converted, sealed, and replaced with proper waste points.

Citizens adopt permanent hygiene habits

Long-term behavioral change — not temporary compliance with a campaign.
