Mission Clean City

How We Actually Work

Six concrete action pillars, each with assigned volunteers, tracked outcomes, and real accountability.

Door-to-Door Waste Awareness

Volunteers engage directly with households to educate on wet vs dry waste segregation — pushing behavioral change, not just one-time action.

On-Ground Cleanliness Drives

Regular campaigns in neglected areas — streets, drains, markets, empty plots. These are continuous operations, not photo-op events.

Ward-Level Problem Identification

We systematically map areas with zero or poor sanitation service, document with photos and reports, then escalate officially.

System Activation

Using public grievance portals and local authority channels, we ensure the Nagar Parishad actually responds — and we track resolutions.

Community Mobilization

We involve residents, shopkeepers, and students — converting passive citizens into active participants who own local cleanliness.

Volunteer Task System

Each volunteer gets assigned specific areas and tasks with clear accountability — real execution over random social media activism.
