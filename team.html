Team Values

Execution Over Talk

We measure success by what gets done on the ground, not by how many posts we make.

Accountability

Every volunteer has assigned tasks with tracked outcomes — no room for invisible participation.

Inclusive Leadership

Everyone who contributes meaningfully can earn a leadership role — age and status don't matter.

City First

We don't do this for résumés or recognition. We do it because Kishanganj deserves better.

KYC.

Kishanganj Youth Club — transforming our city through direct action, community pressure, and youth power. Mission Clean City is our commitment to Kishanganj.
