Our Process

How We Document Impact

Every action is recorded with evidence so complaints can't be ignored and progress can be verified.

01

Photo Documentation

Before-and-after photographs taken at every drive and cleanup. Visual proof that can't be disputed or forgotten.

02

Written Reports

Detailed field reports filed with ward number, issue type, date, volunteer count, and resolution timeline.

03

Portal Filing

All complaints submitted through official government grievance portals — creating a traceable, official paper trail.

04

Resolution Tracking

We follow up until every complaint is resolved — not just submitted. Every open issue stays on the active list until closed.
