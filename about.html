About KYC

Our Story &
Our Purpose

We didn't wait for someone to fix Kishanganj. We became the people who would.

Vision

A Kishanganj where every citizen takes pride in their surroundings — where clean streets, functional drains, and proper waste management are the norm, not the exception.

Mission

To transform the city's sanitation system through structured youth action — combining community mobilization, data-backed complaints, and relentless follow-through on every issue.

Goals

Equal cleanliness across all wards, daily waste collection as standard, elimination of open dumping, and a culture of civic responsibility that outlasts any single campaign.

Our Origin

Born From
Frustration

Kishanganj Youth Club started with a simple question: Why does garbage pile up for weeks in our market? Why are drains never cleared? Why do complaints go nowhere?

The answer wasn't just "government failure." It was civic passivity — a culture where problems are accepted, not challenged. KYC was founded to break that cycle.

We are not an NGO. We are not a political group. We are young citizens who decided that the change we wanted had to be built by us — ward by ward, drain by drain, household by household.

"Real change doesn't come from awareness. It comes from execution."

— Founding principle of KYC

Problem Areas We Target
Open Dumping Sites

Vacant plots and roadside areas used as unauthorized dump sites with no accountability.

Blocked Drains

Drains clogged with waste, causing waterlogging and disease spread in residential areas.

Irregular Waste Collection

Many wards go days without garbage collection, forcing residents into unsafe disposal habits.

Low Civic Awareness

Many residents lack knowledge of proper waste segregation, worsening the overall sanitation crisis.

How We Work

Our Working Model

A step-by-step civic operation built for real, lasting impact.

1. Identify & Document

Volunteers conduct ward-level audits, mapping every sanitation failure point with photos, GPS coordinates, and written reports.

2. Engage Community

Door-to-door visits educate residents on waste segregation, hygiene practices, and how to use official complaint systems.

3. File Official Complaints

All documented issues are submitted through public grievance portals and to local authority channels — creating a paper trail.

4. Follow Up & Pressure

We track every complaint and apply consistent pressure on Nagar Parishad until resolution — not just submission.

5. Measure & Report

Outcomes are recorded and shared publicly — holding both government and our own volunteers accountable for results.

Future Plans
KYC Mobile App

A dedicated app for complaint filing, volunteer task management, and real-time complaint tracking — powered by Firebase.

School Partnerships

Formal hygiene and civic responsibility programs integrated into school curricula across Kishanganj.

Full Ward Coverage

Expanding operations to cover all municipal wards in Kishanganj with dedicated ward leads and structured task systems.

Replication Blueprint

A documented model that other cities and youth groups can adopt to replicate the KYC operating system.
